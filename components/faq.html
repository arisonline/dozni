Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DOZNI tools, privacy, downloads and browser support.

Are DOZNI tools free to use?

Many DOZNI tools are designed to be available directly in your browser without requiring a paid subscription. Individual tools may have their own limits or requirements.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Our tools are designed to let you start working immediately without unnecessary signup requirements.

Are my uploaded files stored?

Tools that process files in the browser can work locally on your device. Always check the individual tool's privacy information before uploading sensitive material.

Can I use DOZNI tools on mobile?

Yes. DOZNI is designed to work across modern desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.

Where can I find all available tools?

Visit the Tools section to browse categories such as PDF, image, calculator and developer tools.

Does DOZNI provide crypto resources?

Yes. The Crypto section is planned for blockchain networks, explorers, crypto utilities and related resources.
